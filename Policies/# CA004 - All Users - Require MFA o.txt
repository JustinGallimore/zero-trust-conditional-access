# CA004 - All Users - Require MFA on Sign-In Risk

## Purpose
Triggers a real-time MFA step-up challenge when Microsoft Identity Protection detects a medium or high risk sign-in, such as impossible travel, anonymous IP, or unfamiliar sign-in properties.

## Scope
- Included: All users
- Excluded: Break Glass Emergency, labadmin, Justin Gallimore

## Target Resources
All cloud applications

## Conditions
Sign-in risk level: Medium, High

## Grant Controls
Require multifactor authentication

## Policy State
Report-only

## Why This Policy Exists
Not all suspicious sign-ins happen at predictable times. This policy uses Microsoft's real-time threat intelligence to catch anomalous behavior mid-session and force re-verification before access is granted.

## Real World Impact
If a user's credentials are used from an unfamiliar location or anonymous proxy, the system challenges the sign-in in real time rather than allowing silent access.

## Notes
Requires Entra ID P2 license. Identity Protection must be active on the tenant for risk signals to fire.
